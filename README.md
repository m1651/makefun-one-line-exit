A one-line resignation letter for [make.fun's "The One-Line Exit" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-the-one-line-exit-1dc652d1): exactly one line, under 140 characters, written to be as funny as possible.

## What it does

`main.py` writes a single funny resignation letter to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Please accept my resignation, effective today: after three years of 'we're a family here,' I've decided to move out.

The joke turns the office cliché "we're a family here" into its punchline: after three years of hearing it, the employee finally moves out — like every adult child eventually does.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
